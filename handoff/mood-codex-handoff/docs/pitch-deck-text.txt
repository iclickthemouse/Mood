SLIDE 02: Creative references pile up. Briefs still get rewritten.
PROBLEM
Moodboards show taste, but they do not explain it well enough for models, teammates, or repeatable production.
WHERE THE WORK BREAKS
- Visual intent stays vague: palette, lighting, texture, framing, and atmosphere remain open to interpretation.
- Writing voice is hard to transfer: samples reveal rhythm and diction, but rarely become a reusable guide.
- Prompts get rebuilt from scratch whenever the model, project, or collaborator changes.
- The missing layer is synthesis. References should become instructions.

SLIDE 03: Why now
MARKET TIMING
Teams already use AI, but the handoff from taste to usable direction is still manual.
SIGNALS
- McKinsey 2025: 88% of surveyed organizations report regular AI use in at least one business function, but only about one-third have begun to scale AI programs.
- Adobe 2025: nearly two-thirds of marketers expect content demand to quintuple between 2024 and 2026.
- Canva/Harris Poll 2025: 84% say poor visual communication causes delays and confusion; the U.S. visual content economy is estimated at $143B annually.
- Adobe Creators Toolkit 2025: 86% of creators use creative generative AI, and 85% would turn to an AI agent that learns their creative style.
GAP
- Generation is getting easier. Precise creative direction is still hard to capture, reuse, and share.

SLIDE 04: mood makes references operational.
SOLUTION
Collect on an infinite canvas, then turn the board into an output that travels.
TWO BOARD TYPES
- Image board: photos -> image analyses -> one cohesive image-to-text prompt.
- Text board: writing samples -> synthesized skill.md voice profile.
RESULT
- The output can be copied, downloaded, regenerated, or reused in another model or workflow.
- The product shifts moodboards from "look at this" to "make more like this."

SLIDE 05: The product loop
HOW IT WORKS
Boards, canvas, and output stay in one workspace so the creative loop stays tight.
FLOW
- Create: click New, name the board, and choose Image or Text.
- Collect: drop images, text files, selected text, or typed notes.
- Arrange: pan, zoom, and move items freely on an infinite canvas.
- Synthesize: output updates when content changes, not when layout changes.
- Reuse: export the prompt or skill.md as the handoff asset.

SLIDE 06: Image boards turn visual taste into one prompt.
IMAGE BOARD
Each photo is read, then the set is fused into a single creative direction.
WHAT HAPPENS
- Each image is downscaled and analyzed for subject, composition, color, texture, lighting, atmosphere, and style.
- The analyses are synthesized into one prompt, not a list of separate references.
- Add or remove an image and the prompt re-fuses automatically.
- Moving items never triggers generation.
EXAMPLE OUTPUT
- A hushed winter cabin at blue hour, snow-muffled light, warm lamp glow, pine, woodsmoke, indigo and amber.

SLIDE 07: Text boards turn voice into skill.md.
TEXT BOARD
Writing samples become a reusable Markdown style profile once the board has enough material.
HOW IT WORKS
- Drop text files, paste selected text, or add notes directly on the canvas.
- Below five notes, the app shows progress toward generation.
- At five or more notes, it creates a skill.md covering voice, tone, rhythm, diction, punctuation, themes, structure, do/don't rules, and a voice cheat-sheet.
- Edits update the profile, so the skill stays alive as the board changes.
USE
- A portable voice guide for writers, editors, agents, custom GPTs, and model workflows.

SLIDE 08: The live loop stays fast and predictable.
PRODUCT BEHAVIOR
The app separates content changes from layout changes, so synthesis only runs when it should.
RELIABILITY DETAILS
- Content edits are debounced by 650 ms before output generation.
- Async generations are token-guarded; stale results are discarded after rapid changes.
- Pan, zoom, and drag do not trigger model calls.
- Errors surface with a regenerate path instead of a silent hang.
- The current MVP stores work in session memory, so a refresh starts clean.

SLIDE 11: Not a static board. A synthesis layer.
DIFFERENTIATION
The core product is not storage. It is conversion from references to reusable direction.
EDGE
- Static moodboards store references. mood converts references into instructions.
- Prompt tools start from text. mood starts from actual image boards and writing samples.
- Manual style guides are slow. mood creates a working skill.md from examples.
- The canvas supports spatial thinking while the output panel stays live.
- The deliverable is the prompt, the style guide, or the brief - not just the board.
